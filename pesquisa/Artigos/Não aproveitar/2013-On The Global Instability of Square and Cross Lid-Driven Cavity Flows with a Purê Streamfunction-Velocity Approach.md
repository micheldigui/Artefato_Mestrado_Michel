## ABSTRACT

This manuscript embodies an investigation of flow stabilities in lid-driven cavities of square (one-sided) and cross (two-sided) geometry with the scheme developed by Kalita and Gupta, which was derived by using the pure streamfunctionvelocity (ψ -v) formulation of the 2-D incompressible Navier-Stokes' equations. In the current work, for both the geometries, the authors, for the first time globally analyse the flow stability utilizing the cited scheme which was  based on pure streamfunction-velocity formulation. They also perform a validation study of the applied scheme on the two-sided cross lid-driven cavity flow through a comparison exercise for  a a certain range of Reynolds  numbers  and excellent  comparison with established results have been obtained. Critical parameters have been studied with the help of eigenvalue analysis and results have been reported. It has been observed that there is a reasonable agreement with established results for the square cavity, while for the two-sided cross cavity, it's global stability analysis has been carried out for the first time and new results are obtained.

lid-driven, geometry, global stability, navier-stokes, higher order compact, accuracy.

## 1. INTRODUCTION

Dynamics of fluid flows in lid-driven cavities (LDC) over the  last few decades  has  been most  extensively studied. It  has  been the  most  widely used benchmark problem for the  assessment  of numerical  algorithms developed for fluid motion governed by the  NavierStokes  equations. Literatures  suggest  that  the  global stability of the  one-sided square  lid-driven cavity has been analysed by numerous researchers and various techniques have been employed with several established numerical methods [2-8].
